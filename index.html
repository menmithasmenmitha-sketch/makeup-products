How it's built

Every BLOOM BEAUTY shade is mixed from the same four pigments — that's why nothing you pair ever clashes.

One

Pigment first

We start each shade from raw pigment, not a pre-mixed base, so color stays true from bottle to skin.

Two

Skin-tested in daylight

Every batch is checked under natural light across a range of skin tones before it ships.

Three

Formulated to move

Talc-free, wax-free bases that flex with your expression instead of cracking by noon.

Four

One family, always

Lip, cheek, and lid shades are cross-matched on release, so your look is never an accident.

Our Story

Where Bloom Beauty started, and where it's headed.

Everything started with an idea of creating products that could make everyone feel confident regardless of their skin color, style, and even budget.

We have created this store for people who love makeup because they think that beauty can be exciting and effortless.

Each product in our store is carefully selected to allow you to feel confident and express yourself in your daily looks.
